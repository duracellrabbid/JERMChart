### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment:**
Grounded Fiduciary Grammar Wrapped in a Generic SaaS Shell. JERMChart embeds genuine corporate trust and fiduciary governance taxonomy directly into its spatial geometry. Representing Trusts as apex triangles, corporate vehicles as structured rectangular cards, banks as circular depository nodes, and governance roles as docked satellite cards is domain-native craft. However, the enclosing application shell dilutes this authority: standard dark slate header, button soup, and missing visual notation legend.

**Deterministic Scan:**
The CLI scan flagged 3 findings across 1 file (`border-accent-on-rounded` in `src/components/sidebar/SidebarInspector.tsx` lines 32, 42, 53 on tab buttons combining `rounded-t-md` with `border-b-2`). 0 violations across the rest of the application codebase.

### Priority Issues (P0–P3)

#### [P1] Missing On-Canvas and Exportable Notation Legend
- **Why it matters**: Fiduciary charts are presented to outside trustees, banks, tax auditors, and family office principals who will not intuitively know the custom visual grammar (triangles, circles, dashed governance links).
- **Fix**: Add a floating, collapsible canvas Legend dock (bottom-left) explaining entity shapes and line conventions, with a toggle to stamp it into exported PDFs/PPTXs.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable layout

#### [P1] App Header Control Congestion & Flattened Hierarchy
- **Why it matters**: The right cluster of AppHeader.tsx jams 8 adjacent controls with 4 competing button styles into a 56px bar, violating Cowan's working memory rule (<=4 visible items).
- **Fix**: Distill the header into Document Identity (Left), Layout/Align (Center), and Primary Workflow actions (Import, Export Chart, Settings) (Right). Relocate viewport-only controls into a canvas viewport dock.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable distill

#### [P2] Modal Aesthetic & Theming Inversion
- **Why it matters**: ConfirmationModal and AlertModal use dark slate backgrounds, while ExportModal and ExcelImportModal use bright white card bodies, creating a disjointed feel.
- **Fix**: Standardize all modal dialogs onto a unified executive design system.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable polish

#### [P2] Absence of Canvas Undo/Redo & State Persistence Cues
- **Why it matters**: While Photo OCR has an undo snapshot, manual chart manipulation has no Undo/Redo history (Ctrl+Z), causing data-loss anxiety during live drafting.
- **Fix**: Wire a Zustand history buffer for canvas state changes bound to Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, and add a subtle local save status chip.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable harden

#### [P3] Tab Bar Border-Corner Collision
- **Why it matters**: In SidebarInspector.tsx, the tab bar buttons combine rounded-t-md with border-b-2, causing corner seam clipping.
- **Fix**: Refactor tabs to use a modern pill segmented-control pattern or properly offset underline indicator tabs.
- **Suggested command**: /impeccable polish
