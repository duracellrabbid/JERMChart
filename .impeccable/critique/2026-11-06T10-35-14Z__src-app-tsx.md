2. **Algorithmic Structural Integrity & Reversibility**: Built-in real-time cycle detection blocks illegal corporate parent-child loops before they can be drawn. The full Undo/Redo stack and OCR Review Banner provide total psychological safety for high-stakes modeling.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Canvas-to-Inspector Selection Disconnect**
  - **Why it matters**: Clicking an entity card on the canvas selects it with a sky-blue halo, but leaves the Sidebar Inspector on whatever tab was previously open (e.g., Tree or Directors), forcing a redundant second click just to inspect or edit the selected entity.
  - **Fix**: Update the canvas entity selection handler to automatically activate the `'details'` tab in the sidebar whenever a node is selected.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P1] Spatial Collision: ReactFlow Controls & Floating Fiduciary Legend**
  - **Why it matters**: ReactFlow's default navigation and zoom control pill mounts at `bottom-left`, directly crowding and overlapping the floating `FiduciaryLegend` docked at `bottom-4 left-4`.
  - **Fix**: Relocate ReactFlow `Controls` to `bottom-right` (stacked neatly above the `MiniMap`) or integrate the legend toggle into the canvas header bar.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P2] Choice Saturation in Header Toolbar and Export Modal**
  - **Why it matters**: The header right cluster displays 8 uniform buttons (`Undo`, `Redo`, `View Options`, `Auto-Tidy`, `Reset / New`, `Import`, `Export Chart`, `Settings`), and the `ExportModal` presents 7 flat options without visual hierarchy, violating the minimal choices rule (≤4 options at decision points).
  - **Fix**: Reorganize `ExportModal` into two distinct sections: **"Executive Deliverables"** (PDF with "Recommended for Board Decks" badge, PowerPoint) and **"Data & Statutory Records"** (Excel Table, PNG, SVG, Encrypted Backup). Group header actions into logical pill clusters.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P2] Zero-Knowledge Encryption Advisory Gap in Backup Dialog**
  - **Why it matters**: Fiduciary trust administrators safeguarding multi-million dollar client structures must be explicitly warned that client-side AES-256-GCM encryption is zero-knowledge and passphrases cannot be recovered if lost.
  - **Fix**: Add a prominent security badge in `PasswordPromptModal`: *"Zero-Knowledge Security: JERMChart cannot recover lost passphrases. Store this key in your firm's credential vault."*
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`
